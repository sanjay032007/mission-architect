        MISSION ARCHITECT // SYSTEM DOSSIER & CREDITS

        Space Mission Design Game

        Mission Architect is an educational aerospace engineering browser simulation built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 (Challenge: Space Mission Design Game). Players balance critical aerospace trade-offs—mass, electrical power, capital budget, downlink bandwidth, and mission lifetime—to design, launch, and manage real scientific missions to Earth orbit, the Moon, or Mars.

        Evaluation & Scoring Matrix (100 Pts Base)

        Missions undergo an automated post-flight evaluation across four aerospace pillars, plus an optional Team Sync bonus in collaborative crew mode.

        Category Max Pts Evaluation Criteria
        Mission Objectives 40 pts Meeting target orbit profile, installing required primary science sensor suites, and matching design lifetime.
        Science Return 25 pts Sensor resolution, data generation rate, and complete RF downlink closure with ground stations.
        Engineering Efficiency 20 pts Rocket payload margin safety, budget headroom remaining, and electrical power generation vs draw.
        Subsystem Reliability 15 pts Redundant avionics, thermal control loops, radiation hardening, and surviving space weather anomalies.
        Team Sync Bonus +10 pts max Awarded in Team Crew Mode when all 4 crew roles achieve unanimous consensus (Score capped at 100).
